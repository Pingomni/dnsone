Licences from your PingOmni account: the cluster size, and renewals that arrive by themselves.

## What changes

- **Keys from your PingOmni account say how many servers they cover.** A DNSOne Pro licence from the account is a
  2-server cluster (a primary and a replica); joining, or *Add server*, beyond it is refused, as for 1.5.12 keys,
  and now points to *Add servers* on the licence's page in your account. The servers already in keep running, and
  DNS never changes because of a licence.
- **More servers without a new key to paste.** After *Add servers* (or a renewal) is paid in your account, the
  server installs the updated key by itself at its next check-in (within 12 hours, or `dnsone-ctl license
  check-in` now).
- **Fixed:** 1.5.11 and 1.5.12 only took a key from the account when it kept the licence id, and the account gives
  a renewed key a new one: such keys are now installed, once their signature is checked.
- Keys naming a server ID (1.5.12) work as before; keys naming neither a server nor a count still cover any number.

## Upgrading from 1.5.12

`sudo dnsone-update` (or *Settings → Overview → Software update*): no database or system changes.
