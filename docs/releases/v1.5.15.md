DNSOne's new mark: the Tree, from the PingOmni family.

## What changes

- **A new mark.** DNSOne's logo joins PingOmni Monitor's Pulse and RadiusOne's Key: a ring open where the line
  leaves, one stroke, and the teal ping in the same place. A name is looked up down the tree; one path comes out as
  the answer. Its ink follows the text colour, so it reads in the light and the dark theme. New favicon.
- Nothing else changes: no database migration, and DNS keeps answering throughout the update.

## Installing

- *Settings → Software update* → **Install**, or `sudo dnsone-update`.
