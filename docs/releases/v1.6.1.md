Your servers, listed in your PingOmni account.

## What changes

- **My servers.** When the server checks in with your PingOmni account (twice a day, once registered), it now says
  which DNSOne version it runs. *Servers* in [account.pingomni.com](https://account.pingomni.com/servers) lists each
  of your servers with its version, whether an update is waiting, and when it last checked in; you can give each one
  a name there.
- Nothing else changes: no database migration, and DNS keeps answering throughout the update.

## Installing

- A new server: `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Pingomni/dnsone/main/install.sh -o install.sh`, then
  `sudo bash install.sh`.
- A running server: *Settings → Software update* → **Install**, or `sudo dnsone-update`.
