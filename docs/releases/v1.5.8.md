## What changes

- **New: See list.** *Ad-blocking → Lists → See list* shows the domains of a list's last download, 100 at a time,
  with a search (part of a domain, e.g. `doubleclick`) that runs on the server, so lists of millions of
  domains open at once. **Download .txt** saves the whole list. Everyone who can see the lists can use it.

## Upgrading from 1.5.7

`sudo dnsone-update` (or *Settings → Overview → Software update*): no database or system changes.
