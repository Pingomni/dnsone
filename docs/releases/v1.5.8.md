A new dashboard with charts, and See list for blocklists.

## What changes

- **New: the dashboard shows your traffic in charts.**
  - *Queries over the last 24 hours*: answered and blocked queries every 10 minutes, with a tooltip at the
    pointer and a table view.
  - *Clients over the last 24 hours*: the seven busiest clients per hour, the rest as *Other*.
  - Rings for *Query types*, *Replies* and *Answers*, with counts and percentages.
  - *Top domains*, *Top blocked domains* and *Top clients*: each opens the query log for it.

  Light and dark themes have their own chart colours, checked for colour blindness. The client and domain
  charts are shown only to those who may see the query log.
- **New: See list.** *Ad-blocking → Lists → See list* shows the domains of a list's last download, 100 at a time,
  with a search (part of a domain, e.g. `doubleclick`) that runs on the server, so lists of millions of
  domains open at once. **Download .txt** saves the whole list. Everyone who can see the lists can use it.

## Upgrading from 1.5.7

`sudo dnsone-update` (or *Settings → Overview → Software update*): no database or system changes.
