A clearer Service health on the dashboard.

## What changes

- **Service health moved under the key figures and reads at a glance.** One line says *All 17 checks pass*, or
  names the problems and warnings. Below it, each check sits in its group (DNS service, DNSOne, Server) on one
  line, with ✓, ! or × and a short detail. A failing check shows its full message under its name.

## Upgrading from 1.5.8

`sudo dnsone-update` (or *Settings → Overview → Software update*): no database or system changes.
